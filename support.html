Tiny Zoom – Support

Need help with Tiny Zoom, found a bug, or have an idea? Email adi.einy.k@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

FAQ

How do I steer? Use the left thumb stick to steer and the right side to accelerate, brake and fire your power-up.

Which vehicle is fastest? All five RC vehicles have equal stats – grab mystery crates and hit turbo pads to win!

Do I need internet? No. Tiny Zoom works completely offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts.

What data do you collect? None. See our Privacy Policy.
