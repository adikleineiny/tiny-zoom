Tiny Zoom – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short: Tiny Zoom does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains how the Tiny Zoom game for iOS and Android (“Tiny Zoom”, “the game”) handles information. Tiny Zoom is made by Adi Klein, an independent developer (“I”, “me”).

1. Information I collect

None. Tiny Zoom does not ask for, collect or transmit any personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID. There is no sign-up and no account.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your progress and settings (for example, best times, sound and control preferences), the game stores a small amount of game data only on your device. I can't see this data and it is never sent to me. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. Depending on your device settings, your phone's own system backup (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include it. Apple or Google handles those backups under their own privacy policies.

3. Advertising, analytics and third parties

Tiny Zoom shows no ads and contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking tools. I do not share or sell information with anyone, because I do not have any to share.

4. Internet connection and online play

Tiny Zoom plays fully offline. You race against computer-controlled (AI) cars. The current version has no online play and does not send any information over the internet.

5. In-app purchases

Tiny Zoom is free and has no in-app purchases.

6. Children’s privacy

Tiny Zoom is designed to be enjoyed by players of all ages, including children. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country, such as under 16 in some EU countries).

  • No accounts, no sign-ups and no requests for a child’s name, email, photo or location.
  • No advertising, no behavioral tracking and no analytics.
  • No chat or way to contact strangers inside the game.
  • No in-app purchases, and no links that take a child out of the game to buy things or share on social media.

This approach is designed to be consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR, including their rules on children’s data), and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent me personal information, for example by emailing me, contact me at the address below and I will delete it promptly.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or state privacy laws in the U.S.), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Since Tiny Zoom does not collect personal data, there is normally nothing to access or delete. If you email me, I will use your message only to reply, and I will delete it on request.

8. Security

Because no personal information leaves your device, there is no personal data on my side that could be exposed.

9. Changes to this policy

If Tiny Zoom changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online play, analytics or ads, I will update this page, change the “Last updated” date above and, where required by law, ask for consent first (including parental consent for children).

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in Tiny Zoom? Email adi.einy.k@gmail.com.
