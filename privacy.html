Tiny Zoom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Tiny Zoom ("the app") is developed by Adi Klein. Your privacy matters, so the app is built to collect nothing.

Data we collect

None. Tiny Zoom does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only your chosen control scheme (e.g. joystick layout) locally on your device so it is remembered next time. This setting never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Network access

The game works fully offline and makes no network requests during play.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is suitable for players of all ages and collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
